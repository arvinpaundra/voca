Washi Minimal

Quiet paper, sumi ink, a single vermilion seal. Space is the main material; the word is the only thing that matters.

  • Ma (間) — spaceGenerous spacing; one idea per screen.
  • Ink + sealNear-black for action, vermilion only for brand and errors.
  • Mincho for termsBrush-derived serif honours CJK letterforms.
  • Quiet feedbackButtons change only their background on hover. Nothing moves on click.
