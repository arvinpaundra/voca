Word font: CJK characters only, and “Kana”

The chosen word font changes only kanji, kana, hanzi and hangul, character by character. Latin letters (pinyin included), digits and punctuation keep the app font. The Japanese reading label becomes “Kana”.

Highlighted on this page only: the characters that take the chosen style. The app shows no highlight.

Which characters change (Handwriting chosen)

Before: a Japanese field drew everything in the style, Latin letters and digits included. After: only CJK characters change. A CJK character in a Latin-language field, such as a note written in English, takes the style too. It uses the face for the deck’s term language.
