Daily new cards: a target, not a limit

“New words per day” becomes New cards per day, naming what it always counted: a card is one word in one drill. It turns into a target to track, never a limit. Sessions keep filling with new cards, below, at or past the target, as long as the deck has some. The dashboard keeps its look; its “new” figure counts the new cards still available, and its headline says “new cards”.

The rules the screens below follow
  • A card is one word in one drill; each counts on its first review.
  • Target 30 · 12 new cards today → setup shows “12 of 30”; a session still fills with new cards up to its size.
  • 30 of 30 reached → nothing changes in the session; the count goes on past it (34 of 30) and says “target reached”.
