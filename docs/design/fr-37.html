Delete account

A Learner can delete their account from Settings. Before anything happens, a confirmation asks them to type their email. Deleting removes their decks as a deck delete does (FR-2), public ones too. It removes their name, email, photo and Google link from Voca and signs them out on every device. Study history stays, tied to nothing that identifies them. Signing in again with the same Google account starts a new, empty account.

The rules the screens below follow
  • The action lives in one place: a Danger zone card at the bottom of Settings.
  • “Delete account” stays disabled until the typed text matches the account’s email. Spaces around it and letter case are ignored.
  • Deleting is immediate and permanent. Nothing restores it.
  • Forks other Learners made of a public deck stay theirs, unchanged.

What other Learners see

  • The deleted Learner’s public decks leave Community at once. A link to one shows the existing “not found” page.
  • A fork of one of those decks keeps its words and progress. Its “Forked from” line behaves as it already does for a deleted deck.
